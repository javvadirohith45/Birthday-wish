02 / OUR STORY

Five years.
Still you.

Somehow, five years became thousands of little moments — and every one of them means something to me.

Nanaaaaa… ❤️🥹

First of all… happiest birthday to the most special person in my life. ❤️ To the person who has been a beautiful part of my world for the last 5 years. To my love, my best friend, my comfort, my happiness… and the person I always want beside me. 🫂❤️

Ee roju nee birthday nana… so naa heart nunchi okkate korukuntunna. Ee birthday nunchi nee life lo inka ekkuva happiness ravali… nuvvu korukunna dreams anni one by one nijam avvali… nuvvu work chese prathi thing lo success ravali… nee heart eppudu peaceful ga undali… nee health bagundali.

And most importantly… nee face meeda aa smile eppudu ilane undali. 🥹❤️

Because you genuinely deserve to be happy, nana. ❤️

Manam ippatiki kalisi 5 years avutundi ani alochisthe naaku oka different feeling vastundi. Asalu time ela pass ayindo kuda teliyadu.

Ee 5 years lo enno moments vachayi… enno memories create cheskunnam… enno navvukunnam… enno silly things chesam… enno fights cheskunnam… misunderstandings vachayi… konni bad moments kuda unnayi. ❤️‍🩹

Kaani ivanni madhyalo oka thing maatram eppudu change avvaledu… nee meeda naaku unna prema. ❤️

Adi konchem kuda taggaledu nana… roju rojuki inka perigindhe thappa. 🥹❤️
